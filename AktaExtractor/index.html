AktaExtractor

This tool extracts measured protein concentrations from your Cytiva Unicorn program for your Äkta SEC. Usage:

  1. When viewing results in Unicorn, select your wanted peak and enter its extinction coefficient
  2. Close and save the result
  3. Select all your results -> right-click -> Export. This will create a bunch of .zip files.
  4. Upload these .zip files here. You will get an output table which you can copy and paste into your spreadsheet.
    • label: The name of your sample before the brackets
    • ExtCoeff: The extinction coefficient you entered in step 1
    • ExtCoeffAmount [mg]: The mass of protein in the peak
    • ExtCoeffConc [mg/mL]: The concentration of protein in the peak
  5. You can switch between decimal point and decimal comma as the decimal separator, to meet the needs of your spreadsheet's language.
