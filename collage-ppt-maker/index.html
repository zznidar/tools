Collage PPT maker

Load a `.pptx` file, read `ppt/slides/slide1.xml`, preview the actual slide pictures as a collage, then download a new `.pptx` with updated positions written back into the slide XML.

2.8 %
8 px

Click two tiles in the preview to swap them. The first slide is used as the source, and its picture positions are rewritten before the file is packed again.

All processing happens locally in your browser.
